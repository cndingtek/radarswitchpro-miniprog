RadarSwitch is a powerful Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) debugging assistant designed for developers and hardware enthusiasts.

Key Features:
- **Scanner**: Quickly discover nearby BLE devices with real-time RSSI signal strength monitoring.
- **Connection**: Stable connection to peripheral devices for detailed inspection.
- **Service Explorer**: Browse GATT services and characteristics intuitively.
- **Data Interaction**: Read and write characteristic values easily (Hex/Text formats).
- **Log Console**: Real-time log monitoring for debugging connection events and data transfer.
- **Device Management**: Save favorite devices for quick access.

Whether you are developing IoT devices or debugging smart hardware, RadarSwitch makes the process simple and efficient.
